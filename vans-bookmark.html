VANS AND DRIVERS BOOKMARK

Shows today's van number, reg and driver from the Warrior Ops rota page.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar in Chrome (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button up onto the bookmarks bar:
    VANS
  3. Open the Warrior Ops Rota page and log in as normal.
  4. Click VANS in your bookmarks bar. A box appears with the list, a date picker and three buttons: VANS copies the van list (Van Allocation box), STANDBY copies the standby drivers (Standby pop-up) and SAME DAY copies every driver on a working service type, plus anyone with a van whatever their code (Same Day pop-up). Each one copies to your clipboard, then you paste

If dragging does not work, right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page, name it Vans and paste the text below as the URL.
